# Code of Conduct

PhotoHub follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, give and accept constructive feedback, and do not harass anyone. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributors that do not follow it.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through a [GitHub security advisory](../../security/advisories/new) of this repository or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially.
